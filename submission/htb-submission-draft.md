# HTB Sherlock submission — draft

**Title:** CENTINELL — The Invisible Intruder

**Category:** Digital Forensics / Incident Response

**Proposed difficulty:** Hard (subject to expanded artifacts and independent testing)

**Synopsis:** A synthetic multi-host investigation in which a stealthy actor uses seemingly legitimate administration channels. Analysts must correlate authentication, service installation, SSH, DNS, and firewall traces and distinguish supported findings from speculation.

**Deliverables pending:** detailed private instructor walkthrough, verified answer proofs, additional realistic artifacts, independent playtest, and packaging against current HTB submission requirements.
