## Learning levels (curriculum, not HTB ratings)

- **100 — Foundations:** identify artifacts, normalize UTC and verify baselines.
- **200 — Applied:** correlate endpoint, identity, email and network evidence.
- **300 — Advanced:** distinguish stealthy persistence, lateral movement and benign administration.
- **400 — Specialist:** assess cloud, web, IoT and perimeter telemetry.
- **500 — Expert:** synthesize multistage incidents, safe containment and verifiable restoration.

Levels are tags for teaching. They do **not** imply five new sets of folders or an approved difficulty rating. The existing `labs/` topic directories remain the source of truth.

## Promotion checklist: blueprint → playable lab

- [ ] Clear investigative question and fictional scope
- [ ] Case-specific evidence with provenance and UTC consistency
- [ ] Final SHA-256 manifest calculated from packaged bytes
- [ ] Objective, uniquely answerable questions and proof references
- [ ] Private instructor answer key and full walkthrough
- [ ] Safe containment and recovery acceptance criteria
- [ ] Independent blind playtest and correction of ambiguities
- [ ] Rights and recipient submission requirements reviewed
