# Defensive containment decision tree

**Beacon of the Eagle LLC | Centinell Suite Defense**

1. **Immediate safety risk?** Coordinate with operations before disconnecting IoT or industrial systems.
2. **Active gateway impersonation corroborated?** Preserve switch state and isolate the unauthorized port using approved change controls; monitor legitimate gateway availability.
3. **Identity misuse corroborated?** Revoke sessions and rotate secrets from trusted devices, preserving authentication evidence.
4. **Host integrity uncertain?** Preserve disk/volatile evidence and prioritize clean rebuild from trusted media.
5. **Backup trusted?** Validate hashes, recovery point, restore tests, and business owner approval before restoring.
6. **Reconnection permitted?** Require clean credentials, validated network settings, application health checks, and enhanced monitoring.
7. **Evidence insufficient?** Keep a documented hypothesis; avoid destructive remediation without authorization.
