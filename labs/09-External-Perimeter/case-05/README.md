# Case 05 — Remote exploit attempt

**BEACON OF THE EAGLE LLC | CENTINELL SUITE DEFENSE**

**Fictional training blueprint — evidence and instructor answers pending.**

## Mission
Investigate a suspected remote exploit attempt incident at the shared fictional enterprise. Determine timeline, affected systems, confidence and alternative explanations.

## Evidence to produce
Primary: WAF IPS and application crash logs. Also produce independent corroboration, benign baseline, UTC timestamps, source provenance and SHA-256 hashes calculated from the final artifact bytes. No case-specific artifacts are claimed as completed.

## Student tasks
1. Identify first suspicious UTC event with source row.
2. Establish the affected identity, asset and relevant network or host details.
3. Correlate two independent sources and evaluate a benign hypothesis.
4. Recommend the least disruptive authorized containment and justify scope.
5. Propose clean recovery and validation of file integrity, transactions, monitoring and residual risk.

## Instructor gate
Write five unique exact-answer questions only after evidence generation, prepare private answer proofs and walkthrough, then perform a blind timed playtest. Do not publish instructor keys in this repository.

## Safe recovery
Preserve originals and volatile evidence where feasible, protect immutable backups, verify restore points in isolation, validate checksums and application data, obtain owner approval and document RPO/RTO. Zero data loss is not guaranteed.
