# 07-IoT-Operational — Five ethical DFIR cases

**BEACON OF THE EAGLE LLC | CENTINELL SUITE DEFENSE**

These five **fictional** incidents cover internal and external enterprise threats. Each requires evidence-based analysis, approved containment, trusted restoration and validation.

## Case 1: Default IoT credentials

**Investigation:** Identify earliest event, affected identities and assets, corroborating logs, alternative benign explanation, and scope. Preserve UTC timeline, original evidence and SHA-256 hashes.

**Containment:** Obtain incident commander and asset owner approval. Isolate confirmed compromised sessions or systems while protecting essential services and backups. Preserve volatile evidence when safe.

**Remediation:** Determine root cause; revoke affected access, correct security controls, patch or rebuild from verified trusted media as appropriate. Use vendor-approved processes for IoT and operational devices.

**Data-safe recovery:** Stop replication of suspected corruption; verify offline/immutable backups, perform an isolated test restore, validate files and transactions against a known-good baseline, and reconnect in stages with owner sign-off. Zero loss is a goal, not a guarantee; document RPO/RTO and any recovery gap.

**Student tasks:** Submit evidence table, incident timeline, hypothesis assessment, containment decision, recovery verification and residual risks.
