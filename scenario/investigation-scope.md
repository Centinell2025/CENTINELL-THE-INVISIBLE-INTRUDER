# Investigation scope and evidence handling

**Beacon of the Eagle LLC | Centinell Suite Defense**  
**Case:** The Invisible Intruder

## Authorized scope
Analyze only the synthetic artifacts provided under `artifacts/`. Do not scan, connect to, or attempt access to any referenced host or address. All IPs are private or documentation-range placeholders.

## Working hypothesis
Investigators should evaluate a potential chain involving a workstation, a file server, a Linux jump host, and an outbound TLS destination. A sequence of correlated events is not, by itself, proof of exfiltration or attacker identity.

## Evidence notebook template
For each finding record: artifact path, original log line, timestamp, observed fact, inference, confidence, and alternative explanation. Preserve the original files unchanged and hash local working copies before analysis.

## Expected output
An evidence-backed incident timeline, answers to the student questions, a list of unresolved questions, and proportionate containment recommendations.
